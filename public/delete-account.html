🗑️ Delete Your TeamKitty Account

You can request deletion of your TeamKitty account and personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address associated with your account:

Email mpboopathi@gmail.com

We'll confirm and process your request within a few days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email and password) and profile name
  • Your membership on any teams

What's kept, and why

To preserve an accurate financial record for the team (an audit trail teammates and treasurers rely on), ledger entries and game records you were involved in stay in the team's history, but are no longer linked to your personal login once your account is deleted — they remain attributed only by the name that was on file at the time. If you'd like these anonymized further or handled differently, mention that in your deletion request email.

Questions

Reach out at mpboopathi@gmail.com for anything else.
