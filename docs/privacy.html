TeamKitty Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

TeamKitty is a team-expense and fund-management app used by a single cricket team (SDKings) and any other private team that sets one up. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who can see it.

Information we collect

When you use TeamKitty, we collect and store:

  • Account information: your email address and the display name you choose, used to sign you in and identify you to your teammates.
  • Phone number, if you choose to add one.
  • Team and financial records you or your teammates enter: ledger entries (amounts, categories, notes, dates), fee/return calculations, and any receipt images attached to a transaction.
  • Game and practice activity: games you're marked as attending, and practice session participation, entered by a team officer.
  • Team logo images, uploaded by a team officer for their team.

How this information is used

All of the above is used only to run the app's core features: tracking who owes or is owed money, recording attendance, and showing your team's roster and season summary. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside your team.

Who can see your information

Only members of the same team can see that team's roster, ledger, and game/practice records. Financial entries go through an approval step by a team officer before they're finalized. Your email and name are visible to your own teammates only.

Data storage

Data is stored using Supabase (a hosted Postgres database provider) with row-level security, so a team's data is only reachable by that team's own members.

Your choices

You can edit your display name at any time in the app.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account from within the app (Roster/profile area → "Delete account"). This immediately and permanently erases your name, email, phone number, and login, and removes you from every team roster you belong to.

Shared team financial records (ledger entries, seasons, game and practice history) are kept after your account is deleted, since your teammates rely on that shared history for their own team's accounting -- but any entry that referenced you is updated to show no personal identifying information (displayed simply as "deleted user"). No data that identifies you personally is retained.
