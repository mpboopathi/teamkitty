TeamKitty Support

TeamKitty is a team-expense and fund-management app for cricket teams, built for SDKings and available to other teams by invitation.

Need help?

If you're running into a problem, have a question about how something works, or want to report a bug, email us at sdkingscricket@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I join a team? Ask a team officer for the team's join code, then enter it after signing in.

Who can approve a transaction? A team officer other than the person who submitted it (the treasurer's own entries need captain approval; everyone else's need treasurer approval).

How do I delete my account or data? Email us at the address above and we'll take care of it.
